Free NYC Events for AANHPI Heritage Month

May 1–31, 2026 · All 5 Boroughs

Theme: "Power in Unity: Strengthening Communities Together"
Manhattan Parades · Museums · Chinatown · Bryant Park
🎌 Japan Parade & Street Fair Free
📅 Sat, May 9 · 11am–5pm  |  📍 Central Park West, 81st→67th St
The 5th annual Japan Parade with taiko drumming, martial arts, kimono processions & traditional dance — Grand Marshal is manga artist Acky Bright. The Japan Street Fair on 72nd St runs all day with food, origami & calligraphy.
💡 Arrive by 11am for the Street Fair. Parade kicks off at 1pm after a 12:30pm opening ceremony.
🎶 Bryant Park Vinyl Listening Party Free
📅 Fri, May 12 · 3–5pm  |  📍 Bryant Park Upper Terrace, 40th & 6th Ave
DJ GIRL WUNDER spins Mandopop, Cantopop & Chinese folk vinyl from MOCA's permanent collection. Experience what it was like to party in NYC's Chinatown in the '70s–'90s. All ages, first come first served.
💡 Stick around — NY Chinese Cultural Center performs a live showcase after 5pm.
🎭 MOCA: Ribbon Dance Workshop & Live Performance Free
📅 Sat, May 13 · 3–4:30pm  |  📍 Museum of Chinese in America, 215 Centre St
Family-friendly ribbon dance workshop followed by classical Chinese dance, contemporary dance, Eastern & Western instruments, and vocal performances. A full afternoon of culture in Chinatown.
🥁 AAPI Cultural Heritage Parade Free
📅 Late May / Early June  |  📍 6th Ave, 44th→55th St, Midtown
Organized by Better Chinatown USA — marching, singing, dance troupes & cultural floats celebrating all AANHPI communities. Opening ceremony at 44th St. Free to watch along the entire route.
🎵 Asia Society: AAPI Composers Concert Free
📅 Sun, May 3 · 2–3pm  |  📍 Asia Society, 725 Park Ave
Trio Menil performs music by AAPI composers, premiering new work by composer Sam Wu. A rare chance to hear original AAPI classical compositions live in a world-class venue.
🐉 Chinatown Street Festivals — All Month Free
📅 Throughout May  |  📍 Pell St & Mott St, Chinatown
Taiko drumming, Canton Pop, the ceremonial You Shen (God Parade), an Artisan Market, and the Chinatown Cha Cha dance event. Just show up and explore — Chinatown is electric all month.
🎤 K-Pop Night at Lincoln Center Atrium Free
📅 Fri, May 5 · 7:30pm  |  📍 David Rubinstein Atrium, Lincoln Center, 61 W 62nd St
DJ CHEN curates a K-Pop set featuring NCT, SEVENTEEN & Stray Kids. K-Pop cover groups from NYU (KNESIS) and Stony Brook (KBS Dance Team) perform then teach the choreography step by step. A wildly fun free evening — no ticket needed.
💡 Show up early — this one fills up fast.
🎉 Asia Society: AAPI Heritage Month Happy Hour Free
📅 May (check asiasociety.org)  |  📍 Asia Society, 725 Park Ave
Asia Society's signature monthly happy hour goes AANHPI — free guided tours of current exhibitions, drink specials, and discounts at the AsiaStore. A perfect after-work outing with the team.
👗 Asia Society: Prabal Gurung Fashion Talk w/ Lisa Ling Free w/ RSVP
📅 May (check asiasociety.org)  |  📍 Asia Society, 725 Park Ave
Fashion designer Prabal Gurung discusses his life, book "Walk Like A Girl," and AAPI creative journey — in conversation with CBS News correspondent Lisa Ling. A must for fashion and culture lovers.
🌊 "Waves of Legacy" — Chinatown Community Celebration Free
📅 May (check local listings)  |  📍 Chinatown, Manhattan
SNIPES partners with Potluck Club & Phoenix Palace for a community event spotlighting Chinatown's cultural legacy. Free giveaways from local businesses (Banh by Lauren, Art Bean, Maxi Noodles, Yaya Tea), a fireside chat with NY fashion icon Dao-Yi Chow, and sounds by DJ Poon.
🎬 MOCA: Letters from Chinatown — Live Performance Free
📅 Thu, May 18 · 7–8pm  |  📍 Museum of Chinese in America, 215 Centre St
Performers bring to life letters from the Marcella B. Chin Dear family, who came to NYC in the 1800s and built successful businesses in Chinatown. An ensemble guides the audience through Chinese American life in the 1940s–50s — history told through real voices.
🎭 Think!Chinatown: Storytelling Festival Pay as you wish
📅 May 8 + throughout May  |  📍 Chinatown, Manhattan
Opening night on May 8 features emerging storytellers, a personal storytelling workshop, the "Chinatown '75" historical walking tour, and a film series on Chinatown food culture and archival activism footage. One of the most intimate and moving events of the month.
💡 RSVP encouraged for specific events — check thinkchinatown.org.
Brooklyn Barclays · Bushwick · Evergreen Cemetery
🏮 AAPI Night Market at Barclays Center Free
📅 May (check Barclays for date)  |  📍 Barclays Center Main Atrium, Atlantic Ave
AAPI community orgs, food vendors & a live performance by Ajna Dance Company in Barclays' main atrium. The Nets spotlight Brooklyn's diverse AAPI voices — no ticket needed, just walk in.
💡 Great after-work event — grab food and stay for the performance.
👶 Brooklyn Children's Museum: AANHPI Thursdays Free w/ admission
📅 Every Thursday in May · 3pm  |  📍 Brooklyn Children's Museum, 145 Brooklyn Ave
Community Access Thursdays: AAPI storytimes, dance performances & dumpling-making workshops all month. Bring the family — or just go for the dumpling workshop yourself (no judgment).
🪦 Evergreen Cemetery: Asian American History Walk Free
📅 Check NYC Parks schedule  |  📍 Evergreen Cemetery, Brooklyn
Urban Park Rangers lead a 90-min walk through Evergreen Cemetery's beautiful grounds, exploring the connections between the cemetery and NYC's Asian American population. Surprisingly moving and historically rich.
🎤 AAPI Hip-Hop Pop-Up — Graffiti, Music & Culture Free
📅 Fri–Sat, May 5–6 · 5–8pm  |  📍 Bed-Stuy Restoration Plaza, SkyLight Gallery, 1368 Fulton St
The Universal Hip Hop Parade, Chinese American Planning Council & Asian American Arts Alliance team up for a two-night AAPI hip-hop celebration: graffiti art in the park, a Diversity Hip Hop Pop-Up Museum, live music, and community activations. Inclusive, energetic, and free.
🎨 Yayoi Kusama Art Workshop Free
📅 May (check local listings)  |  📍 Brooklyn location TBD
Paint clay sculptures inspired by iconic Japanese artist Yayoi Kusama — polka dots, vibrant pumpkins & organic forms. A hands-on creative workshop celebrating one of the most influential AAPI artists alive.
Queens Flushing · Jamaica · Queens Botanical Garden
🌸 Queens Botanical Garden: Cultural Drop-In Free
📅 Sat, May 13 · 1–2pm  |  📍 Queens Botanical Garden, 43-50 Main St, Flushing
Cultural performances & activities in the beautiful Queens Botanical Garden, right in the heart of Flushing. Pair with a walk down Main St for world-class dumplings, bubble tea & Hong Kong-style cafes.
💃 Filipino Tinikling Dance Workshop Free
📅 May (check Queensboro Dance Festival)  |  📍 King Manor Museum, Jamaica, Queens
Learn Tinikling — the national dance of the Philippines — at the historic King Manor Museum. Come learn the moves, eat snacks & do crafts. One of the most joyful events of the month.
🌳 Flushing Tree Cultural Tour Free
📅 1–2pm (check NYC Parks for date)  |  📍 Kissena Park / Flushing
Explore the Asian origins and cultural uses of Flushing's magnificent street trees — from living fossils to nearly extinct species. Led by NYC Parks Urban Park Rangers. A genuinely unique experience.
🐉 Dragon Boat Festival Arts & Crafts Day Free
📅 May (check local listings)  |  📍 Queens location TBD
Celebrate the Dragon Boat Festival with arts & crafts, storytelling, scavenger hunts, and hands-on zòngzi (sticky rice dumpling) wrapping. Learn about the ancient origins of dragon boat racing in Chinese culture. Fun for all ages.
🚶 Sunset Park Brooklyn's Chinatown Walking Tour Free (donation welcome)
📅 Sat, May 23 · 12pm–4pm  |  📍 Brooklyn Army Terminal, 140 58th St, Brooklyn
A 4-hour guided walking tour of Sunset Park — Brooklyn's Chinatown — covering history, culture, architecture, Industry City (Japan Village!), and the historic Brooklyn Army Terminal. About 5 miles of walking. One of the most eye-opening neighborhood tours in the city.
💡 Bring water, snacks & sun protection. RSVP on Meetup — space is limited.
The Bronx NY Botanical Garden · Parks · Dance
🌿 NY Botanical Garden: Chinese Plants Tour Free w/ garden admission
📅 Select dates in May  |  📍 NY Botanical Garden, Bronx
A serene tour of plants with deep Chinese cultural roots — ginkgo, lotus, bamboo & more. Tours available in Mandarin & English. Garden is stunning in May — peak bloom season.
💡 Combine with a walk through the Thain Family Forest for a full day out.
🦅 Spring Bird Walk — East Asian Bird Cultures Free
📅 May · 9–10:30am  |  📍 Bronx park (check NYC Parks)
Urban Park Rangers guide a spring migration walk in search of birds significant to East Asian culture. A peaceful, educational morning connecting nature and AANHPI heritage in an unexpected way.
💃 South Asian Dance Class — Shape Up NYC Free
📅 May · 1–2pm  |  📍 Bronx park / rec center (check NYC Parks)
Express the joy of spring through South Asian dance with instructor Sloka Iyengar. All movements beginner-friendly and adaptable to all abilities. Part of the free Shape Up NYC series.
Staten Island Allison Pond Park · Clove Lakes
🐍 Chinese Zodiac & Year of the Snake Talk Free
📅 Sun, May 25 · 11am–12pm  |  📍 Allison Pond Park, Staten Island
Rangers explore the Chinese Zodiac and the symbolism of the Year of the Snake — intelligence, mystery & renewal. A fun, accessible outdoor talk for all ages at one of Staten Island's hidden gem parks.
💡 Take the Staten Island Ferry (FREE!) over and make a full day of it. The Manhattan skyline views are unbeatable.
