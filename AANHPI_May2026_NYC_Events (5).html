Manhattan Parades · Museums · Chinatown · Lincoln Center
🎭 MOCA Talks: Oasis in the Great Desert — Wong Chin FooFree
📅 Fri, May 1 · 7–8:30pm  |  📍 Museum of Chinese in America, 215 Centre St
A portrait of Wong Chin Foo (1847–1898), the activist credited with coining the term "Chinese American." A powerful way to open AANHPI Heritage Month — history told through one of Chinatown's most important voices.
🔗 mocanyc.org/calendar

📅 Thu, May 7 · 6:30–7:30pm  |  📍 Asia Society, 725 Park Ave
Poetry, song, and spoken word from Asian diaspora communities — personal stories of migration, identity, and belonging with rhythmic expression and cultural fusion.
🔗 asiasociety.org/new-york/events
🎌 Japan Parade & Street FairFree
📅 Sat, May 9 · 11am–5pm  |  📍 Central Park West, 81st→67th St
The 5th annual Japan Parade with taiko drumming, martial arts, kimono processions & traditional dance. Grand Marshal is manga artist Acky Bright. Japan Street Fair on 72nd St runs all day with food, origami & calligraphy.
💡 Arrive by 11am for the Street Fair. Parade kicks off at 1pm after a 12:30pm opening ceremony.
